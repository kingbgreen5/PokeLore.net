## Canonical policy observed in the source data

- Regional, sex-dependent, appliance, battle/state and certain item forms listed above have their own entries in `pokemonRoutes.json`; this POC preserves those canonical routes.
- Alcremie sweets, Vivillon patterns and Furfrou trims are not separate canonical routes in the registry; this POC does not create them.
- A form route is not automatically an evolution edge. `formSemantics.js` selects regional branch, node replacement, or clarified base relationship while the authoritative chain remains the source of facts.

## Inheritance audit target

The stress run records whether each field is direct, shared by an explicit source policy, or missing. Types, abilities, stats, dimensions and artwork must come from the routed form record. Species prose, Pokédex entries, evolution chains, learnsets, encounters and size corrections require separate auditing because the existing data utilities may share or fall back across forms.
