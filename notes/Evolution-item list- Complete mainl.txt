 Evolution-item list- Complete mainline Pokémon

Using the core-series definition—items that directly trigger a permanent evolution, are held during an evolution event, or are consumed as part of a special evolution—there are **51 unique Evolution items through Pokémon Legends: Z-A**. Methods shown below are their usual methods; *Legends: Arceus* allows many traditional trade items to be used directly from the Satchel. ([Bulbapedia][1])

## Evolution-related item edge cases

These are worth including in a comprehensive evolution database, but they are not normally counted among the 51 formal Evolution items:

* **Leader’s Crest** — Bisharp must defeat three wild Bisharp that are holding Leader’s Crests, then level up to become Kingambit. Your Bisharp does **not** need to hold one.
* **Gimmighoul Coins** — the player must possess 999 coins when Gimmighoul levels up to evolve into Gholdengo.
* **Poké Ball** — a standard Poké Ball and an empty party slot are required for Shedinja to appear when Nincada evolves into Ninjask.
* **TM226: Dragon Cheer** — indirectly required for Dipplin → Hydrapple because Dipplin must know Dragon Cheer when it levels up. The evolution condition is technically the move, not the TM itself. ([Bulbapedia][1])

Mega Stones, Soothe Bell, Incenses, form-changing items, fusion items, and Everstone are excluded because they either do not cause permanent species evolution or only assist, alter, or prevent evolution. Pokémon GO–exclusive items such as the Sinnoh Stone and Unova Stone are also excluded because Pokémon GO is not part of the mainline core series.
